How It Works

From Physical World to Actionable Intelligence

01
📡

Edge Sensing

ESP32-S3 nodes with calibrated temperature, humidity, and power sensors. Battery or mains powered. 4G failover built in. No WiFi dependency.

02
🔒

Secure Telemetry

MQTT over TLS 1.3 with mutual certificate authentication. Data encrypted at the device — not just in transit. NIS2 Article 21 compliant.

03
⚡

Real-Time Processing

AWS IoT Core ingests telemetry. TimescaleDB stores time-series data. Alert engine evaluates rules in under 1 second. SMS, email, WhatsApp notification.

📊

Compliance Reporting

HACCP, GDP, and ISO 22000 reports generated automatically as signed PDFs. Audit-ready in one click. No manual compilation.
